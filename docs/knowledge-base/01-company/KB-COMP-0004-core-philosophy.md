## Principles

- Every trade deserves an explanation.
- Every explanation should make the trader better.
- Discipline and profitability are complementary goals.
- Trust is earned through transparency.
- Education never stops.
- AI should support judgment rather than hide reasoning.
- Long-term consistency matters more than hype.
- The platform should not manufacture trades simply to remain active.
