## Mission

StructureIQ exists to help people become disciplined, consistently improving traders through explainable artificial intelligence, structured research, risk awareness, and transparent decision-making.

Success is not simply producing trades. The platform should improve the trader: their patience, understanding, risk discipline, self-review, and ability to make or reject decisions with evidence.
