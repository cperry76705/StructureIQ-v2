## Vision

StructureIQ aims to become a trusted, institutional-grade AI trading intelligence platform that remains understandable and accessible to retail traders.

The long-term vision is premium intelligence without opacity: disciplined research, clear explanations, modern workflow design, and decision support that earns trust through transparency.
