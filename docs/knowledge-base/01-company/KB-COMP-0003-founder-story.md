## Story

The founder began trading around 2015 and experienced many of the traps that harm aspiring traders: blown accounts, poor mentorship, bad academy programs, PAMM/MLM experiences, misinformation, scams, and a lack of trustworthy education.

StructureIQ began as something intended to help friends and family make better trading decisions. Over time, the idea evolved into a commercial platform: not a shortcut around learning, but a system designed to make disciplined, transparent market intelligence available to more people.
