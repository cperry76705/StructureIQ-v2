## Definition

StructureIQ succeeds when it positively changes users' lives by helping them become more disciplined, educated, and capable of understanding market research and trading decisions.

Revenue, growth, and automation matter, but they are not the deepest definition of success. The durable goal is to help traders behave better under uncertainty.
