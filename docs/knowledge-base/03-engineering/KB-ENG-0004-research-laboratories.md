## Philosophy

StructureIQ creates research laboratories to measure proposed changes without automatically changing production behavior.

Known examples include Threshold Sensitivity Study, Trade Outcome Diagnostics, Trade Management Sensitivity, Setup Coverage Diagnostics, Execution Sensitivity Laboratory, Entry Timing Laboratory, Regime Validation Laboratory, Regime Tuning Laboratory, Regime Forward Validation, Regime Confidence Calibration, Monte Carlo Reporting, Advanced Statistical Validation, Score Engine, Execution Intelligence, Confidence Calibration Engine, and Strategy Rating Engine.
