## Lifecycle

StructureIQ should eventually manage the entire trade lifecycle rather than only entries:

1. Research.
2. Validation.
3. Entry.
4. Management.
5. Exit.
6. Review.
7. Learning.

Trade Intelligence v1.0 makes this lifecycle a signature workspace component. The interface identifies the current stage, connects opportunity observations and alternative scenarios to the active plan, and carries completed decisions toward review and learning. This product approval does not claim that every lifecycle interface or automation is implemented.
