## Context

StructureIQ began amid questions about building an AI trading bot and learning from existing tools.

## Decision

Build StructureIQ's own intelligence instead of copying proprietary competitors.

## Consequences

The platform may learn from public product categories, but implementation, reasoning, branding, and data architecture must be original.
