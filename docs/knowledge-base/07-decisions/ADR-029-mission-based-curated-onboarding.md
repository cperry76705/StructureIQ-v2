## Context

Live markets cannot guarantee a safe, consistent first experience. Onboarding must teach StructureIQ's mission, risk, explainability, workspaces, and authority model rather than function as a skippable settings checklist.

## Decision

Onboarding is mandatory, resumable, and mission-based. It uses curated educational market and trade scenarios, never live opportunities or customer capital. Every user receives a universal learning layer followed by tier-specific education and a guided first success.

## Consequences

- Progress persists when onboarding is interrupted.
- Explorer completes a curated Market Intelligence review.
- Professional approves a simulated AI-prepared trade.
- Elite reviews a simulated Autopilot workflow.
- The symbolic Trading Commitment reinforces discipline but does not replace legal disclosures.
- Scenario content and onboarding UI remain future implementation work.
