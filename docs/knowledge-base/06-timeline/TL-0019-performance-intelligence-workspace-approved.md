## Event

Performance Intelligence Workspace v1.0 became the fourth authenticated flagship StructureIQ workspace. Command Center → Market Intelligence → Trade Intelligence → Performance Intelligence now forms the complete flagship decision and improvement loop.

Decision Quality was established as the primary behavioral KPI. Performance Attribution, Discipline Intelligence, Missed Opportunity Intelligence, and AI Coaching became signature concepts. StructureIQ Edge Score was intentionally retained as CONCEPT / FUTURE pending definition and validation.

The high-fidelity design was approved without requested revision. This approval establishes a product baseline and does not imply frontend or supporting-engine implementation.

## Significance

Performance Intelligence completes the authenticated product architecture and establishes StructureIQ's continuous improvement engine: Trading Behavior → Outcomes → Review → Coaching → Improvement.
