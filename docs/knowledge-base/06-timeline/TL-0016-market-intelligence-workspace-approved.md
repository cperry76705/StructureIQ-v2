## Event

StructureIQ Market Intelligence Workspace v1.0 was approved as an official product design baseline.

Market Intelligence became the third approved major StructureIQ experience after the Landing Page and Command Center.

The first high-fidelity concept was approved without requested design changes.

## Significance

The page established an AI-first market research philosophy: StructureIQ begins with intelligence and uses charts as supporting evidence.

The workspace introduced AI Market Brief, Market Health, Opportunity Radar, AI Analysis, Session Intelligence, Market Drivers, Economic Calendar, Volatility Overview, and AI Observation Timeline as core product concepts.

This decision reinforced StructureIQ's positioning as an AI Trading Intelligence platform rather than a charting platform.

## Product Boundary

This milestone records an approved design baseline. It does not claim that frontend implementation, calendar feeds, news feeds, chart overlays, live market-driver integrations, or observation-timeline plumbing already exist.
