## Event

Reviewing the Landing Page, Command Center, former Morning Intelligence concept, Market Intelligence, and Trade Intelligence side-by-side exposed inconsistent naming and navigation. Some designs used left navigation while others used top navigation, and Dashboard / Markets / Trades conflicted with Command Center / Market Intelligence / Trade Intelligence.

StructureIQ standardized authenticated workspaces around one persistent shell. Morning Intelligence was folded into Command Center as Morning Brief, and Command Center, Market Intelligence, Trade Intelligence, and Performance Intelligence were formally established as the four flagship workspaces.

## Significance

The decision creates one coherent product architecture, strengthens navigation muscle memory, and gives documentation, analytics, support, design, and future frontend implementation a shared vocabulary.
