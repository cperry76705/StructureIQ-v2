## Event

Trade Intelligence Workspace v1.0 became the fourth approved major StructureIQ product experience when counting the public Landing Page and the three authenticated core experiences designed so far: Command Center, Market Intelligence, and Trade Intelligence.

Initial concepts were normalized to the global application shell. Market Universe replaced Universe Summary, AI Focus and Market Explorer became distinct Recommended and Browse All Markets modes, AI Top Pick made the AI's initial selection explicit, and Opportunity Workspace replaced Selected Opportunity. The AI chooses the default opportunity while the user retains override control. Unsupported markets receive a clearly labeled exploratory rather than production-trading experience.

The final high-fidelity product design was approved after navigation normalization and opportunity-selection clarification.

## Significance

Trade Intelligence now has an authoritative product baseline joining opportunity prioritization, explainability, progressive execution authority, lifecycle guidance, and education without collapsing Market Intelligence into the trade workflow or implying that the frontend is implemented.
