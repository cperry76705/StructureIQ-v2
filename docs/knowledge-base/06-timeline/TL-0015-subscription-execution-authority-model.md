## Event

During product architecture review, the StructureIQ subscription model was simplified and clarified.

The Professional tier was retained, but its purpose was narrowed. Rather than serving as a broad collection of superior intelligence features, Professional became the approval-required AI-assisted execution tier between fully manual Explorer and optionally autonomous Elite.

## Significance

This resolved concern that three tiers might otherwise create unnecessary product fragmentation or imply that lower-tier customers receive intentionally weaker intelligence.

The authoritative subscription progression is now:

- Explorer: **Teach me.**
- Professional: **Trade with me.**
- Elite: **Trade for me.**

The previous broader feature-differentiated tier concept is **SUPERSEDED** for current product planning, while remaining preserved as historical context.
