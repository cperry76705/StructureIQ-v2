Create the user account and introduce the 14-Day Guided Evaluation value proposition.

## Key Visual Decisions

- Four-step wizard progress.
- Left-side evaluation promise, flagship workspace cards, What Happens Next, and security/trust panel.

## Key Interaction Decisions

- Google sign-in and account form are available.
- Password strength and requirements are visible.
- Terms and Privacy agreement gates progression.
- Primary CTA: Continue → Secure Payment.
- No Apple sign-in.
