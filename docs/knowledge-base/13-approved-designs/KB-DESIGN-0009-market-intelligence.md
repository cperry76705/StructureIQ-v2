## Purpose

Help the trader understand what is happening across markets before considering a trade.

## Key Visual Decisions

- Corrected persistent left sidebar.
- AI Market Brief appears before chart-led analysis.
- Market Sentiment, Market Health Dashboard, Opportunity Radar, Chart Analysis, AI Analysis, Session Intelligence, Market Drivers, Economic Calendar, Volatility Overview, and AI Observation Timeline compose the workspace.

## Key Interaction Decisions

- Opportunity Radar ranks markets, not executable trades.
- Chart Analysis supports intelligence.
- Market-specific browsing remains inside the workspace, not in the global sidebar.
