## Purpose

Introduce StructureIQ as an AI Trading Intelligence platform and start the 14-Day Guided Evaluation.

## Key Visual Decisions

- Dark global market/globe hero composition.
- Strong blue/white hero headline.
- Floating market chips and Morning Intelligence panel.
- Intelligence Pipeline as a major horizontal trust/education section.
- Lower cards for morning brief, market outlook, opportunities, and events.

## Key Interaction Decisions

- Primary CTA: Start 14-Day Guided Evaluation.
- Secondary CTA: Watch a live morning briefing / explore platform.
- Avoid free-trial terminology.
