## Positioning

StructureIQ should not market guaranteed or fixed daily returns.

Internally, commercially useful risk-adjusted profitability matters. Prop-firm-style constraints are a useful baseline for validating trading discipline. The system should seek every valid high-quality opportunity, not force a required number of daily trades.

Market Intelligence should be positioned as research synthesis and disciplined attention management, not guaranteed performance, a signal service, or a charting substitute with implied outcomes.
