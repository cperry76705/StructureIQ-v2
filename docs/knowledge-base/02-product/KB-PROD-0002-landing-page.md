## Approved Direction

- Mission-control aesthetic.
- Premium institutional presentation.
- No public promises of specific daily profit percentages.
- AI is demonstrated through live market intelligence behavior rather than generic AI marketing.
- Core message emphasizes confidence, understanding, trust, and intelligence.
- Global-market / mission-control visual direction.
- Homepage tells a story rather than simply listing features.

The Landing Page remains public marketing and conversion. After authentication/onboarding, users enter the Command Center and the persistent authenticated application shell; the public page does not define authenticated global navigation.

Approved public entry actions are **Start 14-Day Guided Evaluation**, **Subscribe / Create Account**, and **Sign In**. Guided Evaluation and paid account creation remain distinct calls to action. There is no anonymous authenticated product access.
