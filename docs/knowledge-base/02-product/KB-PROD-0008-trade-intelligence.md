## Mission and Design State

Trade Intelligence is one of the four flagship workspaces and answers: **“What should I do next?”** Its full v1.0 screen remains under active design approval review; this entry does not approve that screen or invent its final layout.

## Shell Assumptions

Trade Intelligence uses the persistent left sidebar, approved primary names, and consistent top utility bar. Account mode and AI Authority use the same product-level placement as other flagship workspaces. Market Explorer is an internal Trade Intelligence control, not a global-sidebar destination.

## Preserved Internal Concepts

Current design work may continue to use AI Focus, Market Explorer, Priority Opportunities, Selected Opportunity, Execution Panel, Trade Lifecycle, Alternative Scenarios, AI Observation Timeline, Learning panel, and the supported / validation / planned market universe. Shell normalization does not change these concepts or Trade Intelligence functionality.
