## Mission

Performance Intelligence is one of the four flagship workspaces. It helps the user understand trading results, decision quality, discipline, risk behavior, and long-term improvement. It answers: **“How am I performing and improving?”**

## Current Boundary

The workspace uses the approved authenticated shell and primary-navigation placement. Its full page design, internal hierarchy, and implementation are not yet defined or approved.
