## Explorer

Signal intelligence with manual execution. AI analyzes and explains opportunities and provides entry, stop loss, target/profit levels, confidence, and reasoning. No automatic trade execution.

## Professional

Includes Explorer. Allows Advisor and Co-Pilot behavior. AI can monitor, recommend management actions, and support assisted execution. Automation can be enabled or disabled within permitted capabilities.

## Elite

Includes Professional. Full Autopilot capability may eventually be enabled. AI can eventually enter, manage, and exit trades within user-defined safeguards. Automation remains optional.
