## Summary

StructureIQ v6.0.7 added calibration analytics over candidate diagnostics. It reported confidence, setup-quality, and score distributions; candidate conversion funnel behavior; rejection waterfall impact; near-miss summaries; and symbol, strategy, and regime breakdowns.

The release was analytics-only. It did not change candidate creation, strategy routing, thresholds, risk management, scoring, lifecycle behavior, or paper brokerage behavior.
